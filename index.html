About Me

The person behind
the strategy

I am a growth-focused Marketing Strategist and Social Media Performance Marketer with over 3 years of experience driving brand visibility, audience growth, and revenue through social media, paid advertising, and SEO. I build structured content systems that attract, engage, and convert — aligning every marketing decision with product goals and turning engagement into measurable business outcomes.

I have managed brands across fintech, fashion, food and lifestyle, beauty, and health education — running everything from full social media strategy and community management to Meta Ads, TikTok campaigns, and technical SEO. Across platforms including TikTok, Instagram, X, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Telegram, I don't just show up — I build presence that performs.

My story started almost as a hobby. I was asked to help because I was available and, honestly, because I am chronically online. I'd always noticed the gaps — brands that could be positioned better, messaging that could hit harder, content that could do more. It began with helping my brother get his brand on Instagram, and something clicked. What started as a favour became a career I am genuinely proud of.

I am also a Computer Science and Software Engineering graduate — which makes me a different kind of strategist. Away from screens, I read fiction obsessively, listen to music every single day without exception, travel whenever I can, and consider the beach my safest place in the world.
